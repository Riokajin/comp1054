About the Song

"No Promises to Keep" is a beautiful and emotional song that fits Aerith and Cloud's story in a way that feels almost surprising, because it wasn't part of the original game at all. Even so, it captures the exact tone of their relationship, that mix of warmth, uncertainty, and the quiet moments where they understand each other without needing to say much.

Hearing it for the first time in Rebirth hit me really hard, because the original Final Fantasy VII was one of the most influential games of my life. The remake series has been everything I hoped it would be, and this song feels like it belongs there naturally. It brings back the feelings I had when I first experienced their story, but with a new emotional weight that only the remake could deliver.

About the Artist and Composer

"No Promises to Keep" is performed by Loren Allred, whose voice brings a really emotional and almost fragile quality to the song that fits Aerith and Cloud perfectly. The music for Final Fantasy VII Rebirth was composed by Masashi Hamauzu, Mitsuto Suzuki, and others, and their style has always stood out to me for how atmospheric and cinematic it feels.

Even though this song is new to the remake series, it blends right in with the emotional tone they've built for the world. The combination of Allred's performance and the composers' style is a big part of why the song feels so natural and powerful in the story.
